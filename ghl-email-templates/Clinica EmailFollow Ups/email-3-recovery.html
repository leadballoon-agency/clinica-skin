This is the email most clinics won't send you. We believe you deserve complete honesty about what to expect — before, during, and after CO2 laser treatment. No surprises.

Before Your Treatment

A topical numbing cream is applied 30-45 minutes before your treatment begins. By the time the laser starts, the treatment area is fully numbed and comfortable. Many patients tell us the anticipation was far worse than the reality.

During Treatment

Most patients describe a warm tingling sensation — nothing more. A full face treatment takes 45-60 minutes, and our doctors check in with you throughout. You're never just left alone with a machine.

Your Recovery Timeline

Days 1-2: Redness and swelling, similar to a sunburn. Your skin will feel warm and tight. This is completely normal — it means the healing process has begun.

Days 3-7: Your skin begins to peel naturally. This can look a little alarming, but it's actually your body doing exactly what it should — shedding the old, damaged layer to reveal fresh skin beneath.

Day 10: Fresh new skin has emerged. Most patients can apply makeup and return to their normal routine. You'll already start to see a difference in tone and texture.

Weeks 6-12: This is when the real transformation happens. Collagen continues rebuilding beneath the surface, and improvements become more visible with each passing week. Many patients say they keep getting compliments months after treatment.

You Won't Be On Your Own

We don't do the treatment and wave goodbye. Every patient receives:

  • A full aftercare kit with everything you need for the healing period
  • Direct access to our team throughout your recovery — call, text, or email anytime
  • A follow-up check-in at day 10 to make sure everything is healing beautifully
