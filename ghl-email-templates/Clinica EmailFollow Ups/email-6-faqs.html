Questions we hear most

Before booking a consultation, most people want to understand exactly what’s involved. Here are honest answers to the questions we’re asked most often, {{contact.first_name}}.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients see significant results from a single treatment. For deeper concerns — such as severe acne scarring — your doctor may recommend 2–3 sessions spaced several months apart. This is always discussed during your free consultation.

Is it safe for all skin types?

CO₂ laser can be adapted for most skin types. Our doctors are experienced in treating all skin tones, including skin of colour. Suitability is assessed individually during consultation.

How long do results last?

Results are long-lasting because the treatment triggers genuine collagen remodelling beneath the surface. With proper skincare and sun protection, patients typically enjoy their results for several years.

What’s included in the free consultation?

A thorough skin analysis with one of our doctors, a SkinCeuticals digital skin scan, and a personalised treatment recommendation. There’s no obligation and no pressure.

What does it cost?

Full face treatments start from £795. Face and neck is £1,300. Hands from £450. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden extras. Your doctor will provide a clear quote during consultation.
