Understanding CO₂ laser resurfacing

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

One of the questions we hear most often is: "How does CO2 laser actually work?" It's a fair question — and the answer is genuinely fascinating.

Fractional CO2 laser works by delivering thousands of microscopic laser beams into the skin, creating tiny channels in the damaged tissue. These channels are invisible to the naked eye, but they trigger something remarkable: your body's own healing response kicks into overdrive.

As the skin heals, it produces fresh collagen and elastin — the two proteins responsible for firm, smooth, youthful skin. The damaged, aged tissue is gradually replaced by healthy new skin from the inside out.

What It Treats

CO2 laser resurfacing is effective across a wide range of skin concerns:

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Acne scars and pitting
  • Sun damage and pigmentation
  • Uneven texture and enlarged pores
  • Stretch marks

One Treatment, Lasting Results

What makes CO2 laser resurfacing different from most skin treatments is the depth of change it creates. Most patients see significant improvement from a single session. But the real magic happens in the weeks and months that follow — collagen continues to rebuild beneath the surface for up to six months after treatment.

With proper skincare and sun protection, results can last several years. It's not a quick fix — it's a genuine transformation in your skin's structure.
