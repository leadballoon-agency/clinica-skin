## Ad Image Guidance

**Best performing creative for this type of offer:**
1. The SkinCeuticals scanner in action (if they have a photo of the device/screen)
2. Before/after split showing what the scanner reveals vs what the mirror shows
3. Dr. Katarzyna in clinic — professional, approachable, builds trust
4. Clean graphic: "FREE Skin Analysis" with clinic branding and 5-star badge

**Avoid:** Stock photos, heavy filters, anything that looks like a generic beauty ad. This needs to feel medical, trustworthy, Cambridge-quality.

---

## Key Messaging Rules

1. **Never mention price in the ad** — the scan is free, that's the only thing that matters at this stage
2. **Doctor-led** is the differentiator — most high-street clinics don't have doctors on-site
3. **219 Google reviews** — use this everywhere, it's powerful social proof
4. **Cambridge** — lean into the location, it signals quality
6. **No hard sell language** — "no obligation", "just clarity", "honest answers" — this audience is sophisticated
